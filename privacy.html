Use of Cookies

We use "cookies" to enhance your experience. A cookie is a small file placed on your computer's hard drive. You may refuse to accept cookies by activating the appropriate setting on your browser. However, if you select this setting, you may be unable to access certain parts of our Service.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and local storage to save your preferences, such as accepting our cookie policy. This helps us provide a better user experience by not showing you the cookie consent banner on every visit after you have accepted it.
